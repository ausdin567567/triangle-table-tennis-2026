Our Story

What Is The Triangle Table Tennis Tournament?

The Triangle Table Tennis Tournament is a day-long event bringing players together for a full day of competitive table tennis. On August 8th, 2026, the tournament returns with a World Cup theme — every player represents a country from the 2026 FIFA World Cup, turning the tables into a stage for national pride and friendly rivalry.

The day is split into two competitions: the Singles Tournament, where 13 players battle through a group stage and knockout bracket to be crowned champion, and the Doubles Tournament, where 6 teams compete in a full round robin.

Whether you're here to compete, watch, or just soak in the World Cup atmosphere, the Triangle Table Tennis Tournament is about bringing people together over great matches and friendly competition.
